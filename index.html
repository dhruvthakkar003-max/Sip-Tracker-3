Strategy Rules (enhanced)

        
    
          
  • Primary gate (normal): Nifty ≤ −2.5% from month / ~1M high
    • 
          
  • Second-touch: prefer 2nd day still ≤ −2.5% (first print = WATCH only)
    • 
          
  • Consecutive downs: prefer 2nd–4th down day
    • 
          
  • RSI ≤ 35 + Stoch / BB / below SMA20 + volume
    • 
          
  • Scale-in (normal): ~⅓ at −2.5%, ~⅓ at −4%, rest at −5% / RSI < 30
    • 
          
  • High conviction: ≤ −5% vs high and close in bottom 35% of day candle
    • 
          
  • HC scale-in: 40–50% at HC trigger; rest at −6% or 5-day low
    • 
          
  • Expiry week: prefer deeper dip (−3%+) or RSI ≤ 35
    • 
          
  • Score ≥ 4 → STRONG BUY · 3 → BUY · 2 → WATCH · <2 → AVOID
